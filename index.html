Go live

Right now this saves to your phone only. Friends who open your link will see an empty pond. Three steps to fix that:

  1. Firebase console → new project → Realtime Database (start in locked mode)
  2. Project settings → add a Web app → paste the config into the FB block at the top of the file's <script>
  3. Authentication → enable Google, then paste these rules and publish

After you push to GitHub, add your username.github.io domain under Authentication → Settings → Authorized domains, or Google sign-in will refuse to open.
